Resource FAQs

Where do I submit completed forms?+
Submit through the student portal, or email the relevant cell. Each form lists its submission route at the top.
Are these resources accessible?+
Yes — documents are provided in accessible formats; request an alternative format anytime through Inclusive Care.
How often are policies updated?+
Policies are reviewed at least annually by the advisory committee; the latest version is always published here.
