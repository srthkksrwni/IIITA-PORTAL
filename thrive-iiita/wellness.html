Mental Wellness Mission

To foster a campus where every student feels safe, supported and able to seek help without stigma — integrating prevention, early intervention and care into everyday student life.

Student Wellbeing Framework

Our framework spans four layers: awareness and prevention, everyday support through peers and mentors, professional counselling, and crisis response — so help meets students wherever they are.

Available Support Services

  • Individual and group counselling with qualified professionals.
  • Drop-in wellness sessions and stress-relief activities.
  • Workshops on resilience, sleep, focus and balance.
  • Referrals to specialist care when needed.

Counselling Resources

Free, confidential counselling is available to all students. Sessions can be booked online or by walk-in, with both in-person and virtual options.

Emergency Support

A 24×7 crisis protocol ensures immediate response. If you or a friend are in distress, contact the helpline or reach any counsellor, mentor or warden — help will follow quickly and compassionately.

Self-Help Resources

  • Guided breathing and mindfulness exercises.
  • Sleep, study and time-management toolkits.
  • Curated reading and audio for stress and focus.

Wellness Events & Campaigns

Regular awareness weeks, mindfulness sessions, peer-support circles and well-being fairs keep mental health visible and normalised across campus.

Wellness Toolkit

A downloadable toolkit equips students and mentors with quick self-checks, grounding techniques and a clear map of where to turn for help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is counselling really confidential?+
Yes. Sessions are private and records are protected. Information is shared only in rare situations involving immediate safety, following clear protocols.
How do I book a session?+
Book online through the portal or simply walk in to the Wellness Centre during office hours; virtual sessions are also available.
Can I get help for a friend?+
Yes — you can reach out on behalf of a friend in distress. The team will guide you on how to support them and connect them with care.
