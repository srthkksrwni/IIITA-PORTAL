Student Mentorship

No one should navigate IIITA alone. Our layered mentorship network connects you with peers, faculty and industry guides.

Peer Mentorship ProgramFaculty Mentorship ProgramMentor ResponsibilitiesMentee ResponsibilitiesMatching ProcessTraining FrameworkImpact AssessmentRegistration ProcessResourcesFAQ

Peer Mentorship Program

Trained senior students are matched with juniors to ease the transition into campus life, share study strategies and offer a friendly first point of contact. Each peer mentor supports a small cohort and meets them regularly through the semester.

Faculty Mentorship Program

Every student is assigned a faculty mentor for sustained academic and personal guidance — from course planning to research direction and career choices — across the full duration of their programme.

Mentor Responsibilities

  • Meet mentees regularly and maintain a supportive, confidential relationship.
  • Guide on academics, opportunities and well-being; escalate concerns appropriately.
  • Maintain brief records of interactions for continuity.

Mentee Responsibilities

  • Engage honestly and attend scheduled sessions.
  • Set goals and act on guidance received.
  • Provide feedback to help improve the programme.

Matching Process

Matching considers branch, interests, language preference and goals. Students may request a re-match if a pairing is not working — relationships, not assignments, are what matter.

Training Framework

All mentors complete structured training in active listening, boundaries, referral pathways and inclusivity before being matched, with refresher sessions each semester.

Impact Assessment

The programme is evaluated through periodic surveys, session logs and well-being indicators, with outcomes reported to the advisory committee.

Registration Process

Sign up

Register as a mentor or mentee on the student portal.

Get matched

The matching system proposes a pairing based on your profile.

Begin

Attend your first session and set shared goals.

Resources

  • Mentor handbook and conversation guides.
  • Goal-setting and reflection templates.
  • Referral directory for wellness, academics and careers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is mentorship confidential?+
Yes. Conversations are private; mentors only escalate when there is a safety concern, following clear protocols.
Can I switch mentors?+
Absolutely. A good fit is essential — request a re-match anytime through the portal.
How much time does it take?+
Typically one short session every two to three weeks, plus messaging as needed.
